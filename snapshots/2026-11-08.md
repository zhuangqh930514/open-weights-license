# Snapshot — 2026-11-08

Second snapshot. 23 flagship models across 12 vendors.

## What changed since 2026-11-06

The tier system was wrong, and reading the actual LICENSE files is what showed it.

Previously every non-OSI license was grouped as `CUSTOM` (10 models). Reading each LICENSE file in full split that group in two:

| License base | Before (tag-based) | After (LICENSE-based) |
|---|---:|---:|
| `std` — MIT / Apache-2.0, no additions | 11 | **11** |
| `mit-based` — MIT text + vendor clauses | *merged into CUSTOM* | **6** |
| `custom` — fully bespoke agreement | *merged into CUSTOM* | **4** |
| `NO_WEIGHTS` | 2 | **2** |

Six of the ten `other`-tagged models ship a LICENSE whose first substantive line is the standard MIT grant:

> Permission is hereby granted, free of charge, to any person obtaining a copy…

What follows is vendor clauses — revenue thresholds, attribution requirements, MaaS definitions. That is a different thing from a bespoke agreement, and the `license:other` tag cannot express the difference.

## The four that are genuinely bespoke

| Model | License | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| `baidu/ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base` | ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base Model License Agreement v1.0 | Also a pre-training checkpoint (280B total / 14B active, 304 GB) — not usable as a chat model |
| `meta-llama/Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct` | Llama 3.3 Community License | Gated repo, regional restrictions |
| `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M2.5` | MINIMAX MODEL LICENSE | Ships as `LICENSE-MODEL`, **not** `LICENSE` |
| `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3` | MINIMAX COMMUNITY LICENSE | Contains MIT text — so M3 is `mit-based`, not `custom` |

Note M2.5 vs M3 within the same vendor: one bespoke, one MIT-based. Vendor identity is not a proxy for license type.

## New finding: filename conventions break scanners

`MiniMax-M2.5` publishes its agreement at `LICENSE-MODEL`. A scanner that probes only `LICENSE` returns nothing and reports `unknown`.

This is a plausible explanation for the large share of `unknown` license values in public model indexes: the license frequently exists, the filename just does not follow the convention. Scanners should probe `LICENSE`, `LICENSE.md`, `LICENSE.txt`, `LICENSE-MODEL`, `LICENCE`.

## Release cadence reality check

Tracking repos created or modified since 2026-11-01 across 14 Chinese orgs:

- **24 repos** had activity, but only **3 were newly created**
- Of those 3, two are fine-tune variants (`SenseNova-U1.5-8B-MoT-TailSteer`, `tencent/Qwen3.8-27B-DSpine-b16`)
- The only genuinely new flagship was `moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro` (2026-11-02)

**Implication for anyone building a weekly digest: there is not enough new-model volume in this space for a weekly cadence.** A monthly cadence is honest; a weekly one would force padding.
